## 💡 CRITICAL SUCCESS FACTORS

### **1. Quality > Quantity**

❌ **WRONG:** 100 generic applications
✅ **RIGHT:** 30 highly targeted + cold emails + networking

### **2. Multi-Channel Approach**

Don't rely on JUST "Apply" button:
- ✅ Cold email hiring manager
- ✅ LinkedIn connection
- ✅ Referral (if possible)
- ✅ Follow-up sequence

### **3. Visa Strategy**

**Be upfront but strategic:**
- Mention in 2nd email (not first)
- OR use "sponsorship_upfront" template
- Focus on remote jobs (easier to sponsor)
- Target startups (more flexible)
- Highlight: "Available immediately, remote experience"

### **4. Portfolio > Resume**

**Prove, don't claim:**
- Live demos > static screenshots
- GitHub code > PDF resume
- Blog posts > certificates
- Metrics > buzzwords

### **5. Consistency > Intensity**

**Better:** 2-3 hours/day for 8 weeks
**Worse:** 10 hours/day for 2 weeks then burnout
